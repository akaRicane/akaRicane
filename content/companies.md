## HOLOPHONIX
**Current employer | Paris, France**
[holophonix.xyz](https://holophonix.xyz)

Spatial audio technology company for performing arts and immersive sound environments. Founded in 2017 by Gaëtan Byk in collaboration with **IRCAM** and **CNRS**, spun off from **Amadeus** (professional audio) as an independent startup in 2023.

### Product
The HOLOPHONIX processor is a hardware + software platform that unifies multiple spatialization techniques in a single environment:
- Wave Field Synthesis (WFS)
- High-Order Ambisonics (HOA)
- Distance-Based Amplitude Panning (DBAP)
- And more

Enables real-time placement, movement, and automation of sound sources in 2D and 3D space. Integrates multiple loudspeaker systems into a single spatial architecture.

### Notable projects & venues
- Philharmonie de Paris — *Montag aus Licht* (Stockhausen), with ensemble Le Balcon
- Venice Biennale 2025
- Les Gémeaux, Sceaux
- Neushoorn, Leeuwarden (Netherlands) — with Funktion-One

## DXOMARK
**Previous employer (2020–2024) | Boulogne-Billancourt, Paris, France**
[dxomark.com](https://www.dxomark.com) · [corp.dxomark.com](https://corp.dxomark.com)

Independent benchmark company providing scientific quality assessment for consumer electronics. Founded in 2008 as part of DxO Labs, separated as a standalone company in September 2017.

### What they do
Tests and publishes scores for: smartphone cameras, selfie cameras, lenses, displays, wireless speakers, audio quality, and battery performance. Scores are widely cited by tech media and referenced in manufacturer marketing worldwide.

Also provides **consulting services** to hardware manufacturers — optimizing performance of prototypes and commercial units before and after launch.

### Audio division
Launched October 2019. Tests smartphone audio across:
- **Playback:** timbre, dynamics, spatial, volume, artifacts
- **Recording:** background noise, tonal balance, dynamics, spatial, temporal artifacts

Philippe joined as one of the first engineers building the audio division from scratch.
