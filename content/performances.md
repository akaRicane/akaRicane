### Kube – Collectif Le Bol *(now Course Poursuite)*
**2023** — Salle du Lieu-Dit, Clermont-Ferrand  
Tools: ASTAR, QLC+, Chataigne

Full scenography design for an electronic music night:
- Lightshow via QLC+
- VJing via ASTAR
- 3m metal cube frame dressed with LED strips driven via a custom ASTAR mode
- LED pattern programming with FASTLed on Arduino
- Wireless communication between booth and stage via UDP
