5-year engineering degree (Bac+5, titre d'ingénieur). Speciality: [Vibrations & Acoustique (VA)](https://ensim.univ-lemans.fr/fr/nos-formations/nos-formations/ingenieur-IS/sciences-technologies-sante-0004/cycle-ingenieur-acoustique-instrumentation-EINVAXX_502/parcours-vibrations-acoustique-va-KWAGX9HO.html)

Backed by the **[Laboratoire d'Acoustique de l'Université du Mans (LAUM, UMR CNRS 6613)](https://laum.univ-lemans.fr)**

### Curriculum focus
- Acoustic and vibratory metrology
- Numerical modelling and simulation
- Signal processing, algorithmics, physical modelling
- Signature identification, diagnosis and decision-making
- Dimensioning of passive and active control elements
- Evaluation and non-destructive testing
- Computer science fundamentals

**Languages/tools:** C, C++, Python, Matlab

## PEPITE — Student Entrepreneurship (SNEE)
**[Pépite France](https://www.pepite-france.fr/) | October 2017 – June 2019 (1 year 9 months)**
**Le Mans Université**

[PEPITE](https://www.enseignementsup-recherche.gouv.fr/fr/poles-etudiants-pour-l-innovation-le-transfert-et-l-entrepreneuriat-pepite-46301) (Pôles Étudiants pour l'Innovation, le Transfert et l'Entrepreneuriat) is a national program
initiated by the French Ministry of Higher Education in 2014. It awards the
[Statut National Étudiant-Entrepreneur (SNEE)](https://www.enseignementsup-recherche.gouv.fr/fr/statut-national-etudiant-entrepreneur-46412)
to students and recent graduates building a startup project.

### What it provides
- Academic referent + mentor for project development
- Access to co-working spaces, resources, and MOOC library
- Possibility to graduate with the Diplôme d'Étudiant-Entrepreneur (D2E)
- Entry into the national "Prix Pépite" competition circuit

### D2E result
**18/20** — Diplôme Étudiant-Entrepreneur (D2E), delivered by Le Mans Université / PEPITE

### Context
Started during the final year at ENSIM (October 2017), alongside founding **Spell Audio**.
The D2E (Diplôme Étudiant-Entrepreneur) is an official national diploma accompanying the SNEE status.
