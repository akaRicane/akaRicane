# Publications & References

## AES Papers — DXOMARK

- **Machine Learning: Predicting Audio Quality for high SPL Smartphone Recordings**  
  AES Convention 155 — Express Paper #130 — Session: Perception  
  October 6, 2023  
  Authors: Philippe Guelen, Dan Zhao, Pietro Terra Pizutti Dos Santos, Arthur Drouadene, Justin Bacle (all DXOMARK)  
  [AES e-Library](https://aes2.org/publications/elibrary-page/?id=22284) · [Poster (PDF)](https://corp.dxomark.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Poster_AES__NY.pdf)  
  CNN model predicting perceptual audio quality scores (tonal balance, treble, midrange, bass,
  dynamics, temporal/spectral artifacts) for high SPL smartphone recordings, trained on
  spectrograms from 121 smartphones (2019–2021). Strongest results on tonal balance, bass,
  and spectral artifacts.

- **Laboratory Evaluation of Smartphone Audio Zoom Systems**  
  AES Convention 154 — Express Paper #61 — Session: VR/Devices/Synthesis  
  May 6, 2023  
  Authors: Dan Zhao, Arthur Drouadene, Harry Maisonneuve, Justin Bacle, Philippe Guelen (DXOMARK, Boulogne-Billancourt)  
  [AES e-Library](https://aes2.org/publications/elibrary-page/?id=22086)  
  Rating protocol for evaluating smartphone audio zoom systems via objective and perceptual
  testing. Primary criterion: ability to isolate the target sound source relative to camera
  focal point and zoom level.
